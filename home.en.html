My Bucket List Journal

Collect your dreams.
Plan your adventures.
Make them happen.

BucketList is a personal app that helps you collect the experiences you dream about – from small everyday moments to great adventures around the world.

Plan, organize, and follow your journey as you turn your dreams into reality.

✨ Features

  • Create your own Bucket List
  • Organize your experiences with categories
  • Favorites and completed experiences
  • Map view
  • Apple Maps integration
  • Statistics
  • Achievements
  • Sync between iPhone and iPad
  • Create and organize travel plans

📱 Available for

  • iPhone
  • iPad
