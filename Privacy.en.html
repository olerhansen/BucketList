Privacy Policy for Bucket List

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Privacy

Bucket List is designed with privacy in mind. The app collects as little information as possible, and the content you add to the app belongs to you.

Information you provide is used to offer the features and functionality of Bucket List.

What information is stored?

When using Bucket List, you may choose to store information such as:

  • titles and subtitles of experiences
  • descriptions and personal notes
  • photos you choose to add
  • places, locations and coordinates
  • websites you add
  • favorites and completion status
  • ratings from 1–5 stars
  • destinations and connections between trips and experiences
  • planned travel dates

This information is used to provide app features such as organizing your bucket list, statistics, trip planning, maps, ratings and sharing.

Local Storage and iCloud Synchronization

Bucket List stores app data on your device.

If iCloud is enabled and available to the app, Bucket List may synchronize data between your own Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit.

Synchronized data is stored in your private iCloud database. Bucket List does not use this information for advertising, marketing or tracking.

Your use of iCloud is also subject to Apple's terms and privacy policies.

Location and Maps

Bucket List may use your device's location for features such as:

  • determining which of your saved experiences are closest to you
  • calculating the distance to experiences
  • displaying places and experiences on a map
  • opening Apple Maps for maps and directions

You decide whether Bucket List can access your location through the iOS settings.

Bucket List does not track your movements over time and does not use your location for advertising or marketing.

If you deny location access, features that depend on your current location may be limited. You can change this permission later in your device's Settings.

Calendar

Bucket List allows you to add planned trips or experiences to the calendar on your device.

Calendar access is used only when required for the calendar feature and after you have granted the necessary permission.

A calendar entry is created only when you choose to add a trip or experience to your calendar.

You can manage the app's calendar access through Settings on your device.

Photos

You may choose photos to associate with your experiences in Bucket List.

Photos are used to display and organize your experiences and may also be included in a sharing image if you choose to use the app's sharing feature.

Bucket List does not automatically share your photos.

Sharing

Bucket List allows you to create a sharing image based on an experience. The shared image may contain information such as:

  • photo
  • title
  • location
  • status
  • rating

Sharing only occurs when you choose Share as Image and then actively select where the content should be sent or saved using the iOS share sheet.

You decide which app or service receives the content. Depending on the services available on your device, this may include Messages, AirDrop, social media apps or saving the image to Files.

When content is sent to another app or service, the handling of that content may be subject to the privacy policy of the selected service.

Websites and External Services

If you add or open a website from an experience, that website may have its own privacy policy and terms.

Bucket List is not responsible for how external websites or services process information.

The same applies to services with which you choose to share content.

Sharing and Use of Personal Information

Bucket List:

  • does not sell personal information
  • does not use personal information for advertising
  • does not use data for marketing
  • does not use data to track you across apps or websites
  • does not automatically share content with other users or social media services

Data may be transferred to services you choose to use, such as iCloud, Calendar, Apple Maps or an app selected through the iOS share sheet.

Analytics

Bucket List currently does not use third-party analytics or advertising tools to collect information about how you use the app.

If this changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Deleting and Controlling Your Data

You may at any time:

  • edit your experiences
  • delete experiences
  • edit or delete planned trips
  • change the app's permissions through Settings
  • delete the app if you no longer wish to use it

If iCloud synchronization is enabled, changes and deletions may be synchronized between your own Apple devices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if new features are added to Bucket List or if the way the app processes information changes.

The date at the top of this Privacy Policy indicates when it was last updated.
