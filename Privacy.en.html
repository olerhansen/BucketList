Privacy Policy for Bucket List Last updated: 04.08.2026 Privacy Bucket List is designed with privacy in mind. We collect as little information as possible, and the experiences you create belong to you. What information is stored? When using Bucket List, you may choose to store information such as: Experience title Description and personal notes Photos you choose to add Location and coordinates Websites or links you add Favorites and completed experiences This information is used solely to provide the app's functionality. iCloud Synchronization If iCloud is enabled on your device, Bucket List can synchronize your experiences across your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit. Your data is stored in your private iCloud account and is accessible only to you. The developer does not have access to this information. Location Bucket List uses your location to: Display your experiences on a map Calculate the distance to your experiences Open Apple Maps with directions The app does not track your location over time, and your location is not shared with the developer. Photos If you choose to add photos, they are stored together with your experiences. The photos are used only within the app and are not shared with anyone else. Data Sharing Bucket List: Does not sell personal information Does not share personal information with third parties Does not use your data for advertising or marketing purposes Analytics At this time, Bucket List does not collect analytics or usage data about how you use the app. Your Rights You can, at any time: Edit your experiences Delete your experiences Delete the app if you no longer wish to use it If iCloud synchronization is enabled, deleting an experience will also remove it from your other Apple devices through iCloud synchronization. App Store Privacy Nutrition Label Based on the current implementation of the app, the App Store Privacy Nutrition Label would likely be completed as follows (assuming no analytics tools or advertising are added before launch): Data Collected No Data Collected Or, if Apple requires CloudKit-synchronized content to be declared: Data Types User Content Photos Other User Content Purpose App Functionality The data is: Not used for tracking Not linked to advertising Not shared with third parties The final Privacy Nutrition Label depends on how Apple classifies CloudKit data for your specific app when you complete the App Store Connect privacy questionnaire.
